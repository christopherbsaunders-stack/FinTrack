Connect your accounts

  1. Make an account at SimpleFIN Bridge (about $15 a year).
  2. In SimpleFIN Bridge, connect your bank, Robinhood, your 401(k) provider, your car loan and mortgage lenders.
  3. Choose New connection → copy the Setup Token and paste it here. It works once.

The token becomes a private access link, stored only on this computer in data/state.json.

Paychex's own estimate is a good start for the income. Your Social Security estimate at your retirement age is on your account at ssa.gov (it shows today's dollars). The 401(k) contributions come from its plan, so don't count them in "other saving".

Assumptions (optional)

Follow home prices

Your home's value moves with a house price index (the FHFA House Price Index, from FRED). Each time a new quarter comes out, FinTrack scales your value by how much the index moved. When you type in a new value, it starts over from that.

A state's index comes out every quarter. For your county, choose "Another FRED series" and enter its id: county series look like ATNHPIUSNNNNNA (for example ATNHPIUS51107A is Loudoun County, VA). They're more local but come out once a year. Find one by searching "All-Transactions House Price Index" and your county at fred.stlouisfed.org.

Loan details

The balance comes from your lender. These are on your statement or your lender's website.

The required payment is the amount due on your statement. If you pay more, the rest goes to principal as extra, and FinTrack shows what that saves. Leave it empty if you pay exactly what's due.

Escrow is the part of the payment for property taxes, homeowner's insurance and any PMI. It doesn't pay down the loan. Your statement shows it as "escrow".
